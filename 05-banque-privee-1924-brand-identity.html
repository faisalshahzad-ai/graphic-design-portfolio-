06 · Guidelines

What to Avoid

  • Medieval clutterAvoid excessive ornamental filigree.
  • Photorealistic animalThe lion stays as geometric linework.
  • Messy feathers & scrollworkThey obscure the 12-column grid.
  • Colourful gradientsUse only Oxford navy, warm alabaster and matte silver.
  • Pixelation & muddy shadowsThey break the deboss micro-relief.
  • Illegible type & clipped bordersKeep exact case for BANQUE PRIVÉE and FONDÉE EN 1924.
